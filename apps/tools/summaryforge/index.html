SummaryForge

Dependency-free agentic content summarizer. Paste text (or fetch a URL via a public proxy) and get a key-sentence summary, headline, and keywords — extracted and ranked locally, nothing sent to a server.

Tip: you can also drop a URL in the box and use “Fetch URL” to pull text through a public CORS proxy, then summarize it.
